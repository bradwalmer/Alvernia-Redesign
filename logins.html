Can’t get in?

For urgent password resets, account unlocks, or compromised accounts, the IT Help Desk lists 610-230-5700 (x2008 on campus). For non-urgent requests, use the TOPdesk Self-Service Portal (opens in a new tab) ↗. Details come from Technology Support (opens in a new tab) ↗. Confirm them there before relying on them.
